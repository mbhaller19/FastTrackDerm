Board-Certified Dermatologist · Founder

"I started FastTrack because patients deserve timely, expert skin care without the months-long wait."

Dr. Haller is a board-certified dermatologist with clinical training from some of the country's leading institutions. She founded FastTrack Dermatology MD, Medical Corporation to make high-quality dermatologic care more accessible through virtual visits for patients across California.
